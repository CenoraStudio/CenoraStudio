Privacy Policy

Cenora Wedding Club / CenoraStudio · Effective 8 October 2026

This page explains how CenoraStudio, operated from Türkiye, handles information when you visit cenorastudios.com or sign in with Google.

What we collect

  • Account data from Google sign-in: name, email address, and profile image, if you approve those scopes.
  • Information you send us, such as atelier or bridal matching requests.
  • Basic technical logs: browser type, pages viewed, and approximate time of access.

Why we use it

We use this data to sign you in, match a request with an atelier, reply to you, and keep the site secure. We do not sell personal data.

Google

Sign-in is provided by Google. Their use of data is governed by Google’s own policies. We store the account identifiers needed to keep your session.

Retention and sharing

We keep account data while the account is active, then delete or anonymize it on request unless a legal duty requires longer storage. Processors may include Supabase for authentication and Vercel for hosting.

Your requests

To access, correct, or delete your data, email cerenersen@cenorastudios.com.
